* protobuf / gRPC
:PROPERTIES:
:UNNUMBERED: t
:END:
https://protobuf.dev/getting-started/pythontutorial/

https://grpc.io/docs/languages/python/

Protocol buffers are Google's language-neutral, platform-neutral, extensible mechanism for serializing structured data. gRPC is a high performance, open source universal RPC framework.

** Features
:PROPERTIES:
:UNNUMBERED: t
:END:
+ protobuf: ~.proto~ schemas compiled to classes; binary, text and JSON formats; well-known types
+ grpcio: clients and servers, unary and streaming calls, deadlines, metadata, TLS
+ grpcio-tools: the ~protoc~ plugin; reflection and health services

*** cl-protobufs
Generate Lisp with the ~protoc~ and plugin from cl-protobufs' overlay:
#+begin_src lisp :exports source :results silent
(asdf:load-system "protobuf-backend-cl-protobufs")
(asdf:load-system "process-backend-uiop")
#+end_src

#+begin_src lisp
(let ((native (merge-pathnames "native/" (asdf:system-source-directory "cl-protobufs"))))
  (multiple-value-bind (code out err)
      (stack-process:run (list (namestring (merge-pathnames "protoc" native))
                               (format nil "--plugin=protoc-gen-cl-pb=~a"
                                       (namestring (merge-pathnames "protoc-gen-cl-pb" native)))
                               "--cl-pb_out=output-file=person.lisp:examples"
                               "--proto_path=examples" "person.proto"))
    (declare (ignore out err))
    (load (compile-file "examples/person.lisp"))
    code))
#+end_src

*** cl-stack
The serdes route, and ~google.protobuf.Value~ for JSON-shaped data:
#+begin_src lisp
(let ((protobuf-protocol:*protobuf-message-class* 'cl-protobufs.tutorial:person))
  (list (cl-protobufs.tutorial:person.id
         (serdes-protocol:decode
          (serdes-protocol:encode (cl-protobufs.tutorial:make-person :name "Bob" :id 8) :format :protobuf)
          :format :protobuf))
        (serdes-protocol:decode (serdes-protocol:encode #(1 "two" :null) :format :wkt) :format :wkt)))
#+end_src

#+RESULTS:
: (8 #(1 "two" :NULL))
